# Why Invalidate?

We invalidate when a major change happens that breaks or makes old data or settings obsolete, like a big system update. We version when we make smaller, compatible changes so things keep working smoothly for everyone, even with new features or fixes. The /* wildcard is restricted to avoid accidentally exposing or messing with important files or data, keeping the system secure and organized. This way, we balance making updates with protecting the integrity of the system.
