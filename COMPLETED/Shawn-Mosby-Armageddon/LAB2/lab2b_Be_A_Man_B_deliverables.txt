3) Operational Policy Paragraph
When do we invalidate? 
We use invalidations as a "break glass" operation for static entrypoints, such as index.html or manifest.json, that do not have versioned filenames but must reflect changes immediately to point users to new assets.

When do we version instead? 
We use versioning (e.g., app.8f2d31.js) for all application logic, styles, and media. 
Versioning is preferred because it allows for instantaneous updates across the CDN without manual intervention, supports easy rollbacks, and prevents the risk of users receiving mismatched assets during a deployment.

Why is /* restricted? 
A global wildcard invalidation is restricted because it wipes the entire cache across all 600+ edge locations simultaneously. 
This creates a "Thundering Herd" effect where every subsequent request must be forwarded to our ALB and EC2 origin, potentially overwhelming the servers and increasing operational costs.
