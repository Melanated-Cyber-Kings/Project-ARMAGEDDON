This system meets APPI requirements by keeping all Japanese medical data stored in Tokyo. Japanese law forbids storing PHI outside the country, so the main database and its backups stay in Tokyo at all times. Even when doctors in other regions, like Brazil, access patient records, the encrypted data travels securely to and from Tokyo without being copied elsewhere. Only non-sensitive information is cached through CloudFront, and compute services in São Paulo remain stateless to reduce risk. Transit Gateway provides clear routing and simplifies reporting for compliance audits.
